# ClinOps EMR

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Users
Hospital clinical staff (receptionists, nurses, doctors, pharmacists, lab techs, billing officers) and system administrators who provision access and maintain hospital configuration.

## Purpose
ClinOps is an electronic medical records system for a teaching hospital: registration, triage, consultation, pharmacy, lab, admissions, billing — plus a dedicated System Admin console for staff, RBAC, audit, and hospital structure.

## Positioning
Task-first clinical software for Malawi teaching-hospital workflows. Clarity and speed under pressure beat decorative dashboards.

## Personality
Calm, precise, clinical green. Dense where operators need data; never theatrical. Familiar product patterns (side nav, tables, sheets) over invented affordances.

## Anti-references
- Purple SaaS marketing dashboards
- Glassmorphism / glow-heavy admin themes
- Hero-metric card grids as the primary admin UX
- Public open signup as the staff provisioning path

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast, visible focus rings, keyboard-reachable tables and forms, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for transitions.
